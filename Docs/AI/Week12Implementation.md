# Week 12: Naval combat and advisor/diplomacy dialogs

Reference: the user's `(V.2) Colonization-Game Production Manual.docx`, blocks 2922–3125 and the code/Inspector images. Week 13 starts at 3126.

## Implemented behavior

- Right-clicking an adjacent enemy ship with the selected player ship starts combat when the defender's Hex movement cost is affordable. Moving, off-map, friendly, dead, boarding-reserved, non-player, non-naval and re-entrant combat are rejected. The manual compares Strength directly; higher Strength wins and equal Strength uses a fair random choice. Its Caravel-versus-Caravel example permits unarmed ships to fight. No additional armed gate, damage rolls, terrain bonus or hit-point system is invented.
- Combat is stationary, as in the manual's AttackUnit snippet. Movement points are checked but that snippet does not deduct an attack cost. Controls lock immediately; Combat Analysis appears after 0.5 seconds and the loser is removed after 4 seconds. Closing the analysis does not cancel resolution. The naval result displays the snapshotted winner/loser names and Okay; it does not read destroyed Unity objects.
- Losing ships, their passengers and cargo are removed. Faction unit lists, Hex occupancy, target references and the selected unit are cleaned up. Acknowledging the result restores selection to a surviving available unit if needed. A later Europe confirmation resets the naval dialog back to Yes/No.
- The King issues the exact Sugar/Tobacco/Cotton instruction at game startup. Colony dialogs explain invalid settlement terrain, fewer than 20 tools and adjacent settlements/villages without charging tools. Only Hardy Pioneers can clear/build. The existing delayed work and 20-tool costs remain.
- Both player land and naval arrivals inspect adjacent units for a faction not met before. ForeignDialog uses that faction's leader and European/Native greeting. Yes records peace and closes; No records war and shows the manual's war message with Okay. Already-met and duplicate pending encounters do not repeat the greeting. Relationship state resets for a new game. The manual does not implement diplomatic AI consequences or a treaty-based attack prohibition here.
- The AI movement dialog shows the year and progress until the AI coroutine finishes. It blocks player commands without blocking the AI's own movement. Dialog requests queue so encounters, Europe confirmations and timed combat results cannot overwrite one another. King/colony/result acknowledgments and unanswered diplomacy use the appropriate button modes.
