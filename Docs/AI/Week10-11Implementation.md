# Week 10–11: Cargo unloading, Europe travel and market trading

Reference: the user's `(V.2) Colonization-Game Production Manual.docx`, Week 10 blocks 2540–2787 and Week 11 blocks 2788–2921, including the code and Inspector images. Week 12 begins at block 2922.

## Scope

- CargoDrag displays product and quantity inside numbered holds. Loading rebuilds cargo icons; matching StockSlots accept unloading. Wrong products, fields/outside town, cancelled drags and closing/switching UI return escrow to its original numbered hold. Empty holds remain empty positions rather than shifting later cargo indices.
- EuropePanel opens with E, closes with E/Escape/header X and closes before ending a turn. Its sixteen StockSlots display Bid/Ask, with six holds bound to the selected docked ship. Inbound/outbound icons display turns remaining; only docked Europe ships can be dragged or traded with.
- Each faction starts with 1000 gold. Europe initializes sixteen independent market stocks of 500 units from ProductData.startBid/startAsk. Selling matching cargo earns Bid; buying loads accepted units at Ask. Only accepted quantities are charged. Market stock, hold limits and available money bound a purchase; full/mixed/invalid drops cost nothing. Cancellation returns reserved market stock and does not spend money. Market prices do not change automatically because the manual does not define an automatic price-change rule here.
- A player's ship arriving at the eastern ocean edge opens the manual's Yes/No question. No leaves the ship in place; Yes adds the existing ship to a three-turn inbound voyage. Ship/passenger occupancy leaves the map together. Arrival after three Endturn calls puts the original ship in Europe. Dragging a docked ship onto the outbound ShipPanel starts a three-turn return. The original ship and passenger objects return at column WIDTH-2, with cargo and faction membership preserved.
- Selection, movement, work and camera input reject Europe/modal interaction, and selection skips off-map units. E and ending turns still work when every player unit is away. Confirmation is modal and prevents an accidental turn or a map action beneath it. Repeated departures cannot create duplicate voyages.
- European starting ship rows use the manual's five randomized separated spots. Returning ships retain their actual ship type; this milestone does not replace them with a new default Caravel.

## UI and asset decisions

Existing TownPanel and its object identities are preserved. Native Editor setup adds EuropePanel, EuropeManager, DialogManager and a ToEurope DialogData asset, creates CargoDrag/ShipDrag prefabs and extends ShipInPortIcon with its hidden turn label. Existing ProductData assets receive configurable initial prices only if they lack valid prices. The town header draws after the other town panels so tall forest sprites cannot cover its money or exit controls.

The following manual dimensions are applied: CargoDrag 64x64 with a 64x32 quantity at y=-16, font 28; ShipDrag 128x128; transit text 128x80 at y=-24, font 42 with the shown AutoSize range; Europe UnitPanel 1920x330 at y=-260; Inbound/Outbound panels 430x330 at x=-745/-315, ship grids 430x200 with 128px cells; PortPanel 480x330 at x=140, ship grid 490x200 at y=20, cargo row 450x80 at y=-120; action ButtonPanel 260x200 at (830,5); sixteen 120px stock cells; yellow bold money text 200x50 at (400,0), font 42.

The naval dialog uses the specified 940x400 panel, 860x160 notification area, 800x160 answer area at y=-100, two 800x80 buttons at y=+40/-40 and the exact English question/answers. Its question has font size 48 with fitting down to 38 to keep the full sentence within the notification area. A full-canvas raycast blocker prevents map input through the modal.

The linked EuropeMockup and wooden dialog texture are absent from this project. The Europe background uses the existing Art Deco screen background, and the dialog uses native colored panels. Existing product/ship artwork is reused throughout; no personal artwork, external images or packages are imported. Missing cargo art still uses the previously documented resource proxies. Recruit/Purchase/Train buttons remain disabled placeholders for Week 13. Naval combat, other Week 12 dialogs and Week 13 recruitment/AI/intro steps are outside scope.

Initial market prices (Bid/Ask) are Food 2/8, Sugar 4/5, Tobacco 4/5, Cotton 3/4, Furs 5/6, Lumber 4/5, Ore 6/7, Silver 19/20, Horses 5/6, and Rum/Cigars/Cloth/Coats/TradeGoods/Tools/Musket 13/14. These satisfy the manual's positive spread, maximum 20, and manufactured-goods premium; numbers not specified by the manual are configurable defaults. Passenger berths and goods holds retain the tutorial's separate capacity models.
