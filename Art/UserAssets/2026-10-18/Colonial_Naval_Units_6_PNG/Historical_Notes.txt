Six naval game icons. Transparent RGBA PNG, 256x256, plus original-resolution images.
Historically inspired game interpretations, not exact naval reconstructions. Rigging and proportions are stylized.
Caravel: early1500s lateen-rigged Iberian vessel.
Merchantman: seventeenth-century Dutch fluyt-inspired cargo vessel.
Galleon: circa1580-1620 Atlantic galleon.
Privateer: circa1640-1670 armed pinnace interpretation. Privateer denotes licensed wartime activity, not a fixed hull type.
Frigate: circa1660 two-decker interpretation; terminology differs from later frigates.
Man Of War: large three-decker inspired by Sovereign of the Seas1637. Man-of-war is a broad warship term.
